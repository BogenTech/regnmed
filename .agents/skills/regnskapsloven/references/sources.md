## Regnskapsloven changes to watch

- Lov 21. juni 2024 nr. 42 (bærekraftsrapportering): size categories moved
  to § 1-5 (micro/small/medium/large), in force 1 November 2024.
- Prop. 106 L (2025–2026) "Endringer i regnskapsloven mv. (forenkling)" —
  Omnibus I sustainability simplifications; submitted 19 June 2026, pending
  in Stortinget as of this date.
- The 2016 Regnskapslovutvalg proposal (NOU 2016:11) for a new act was
  **not** pursued by the ministry; do not code against it.

## SAF-T Financial (Skatteetaten)

- Documentation: https://www.skatteetaten.no/en/business-and-organisation/start-and-run/rutiner-regnskap-og-kassasystem/saf-t-financial/documentation
- 1.40 is the only valid format from 1 January 2027 (documentation published
  31 August 2026). 1.30 usable for financial year 2026 and earlier.
- Schemas, code lists (standard accounts, standard tax codes), examples:
  https://github.com/Skatteetaten/saf-t
- Submitted on request via Altinn form RF-1363.

## VAT

- Rates 2026 (unchanged in the 2027 budget proposal): 25 / 15 / 12 % (+11.11 %
  for wild marine resources). Source: regjeringen.no "Avgiftssatser i 2026 og
  regjeringens forslag for 2027"; Prop. 1 LS (2026–2027).
- mva-melding system integration: https://www.skatteetaten.no/samarbeidspartnere/sluttbrukersystemer/mva-melding-sbs/
  (technical docs on GitHub). Altinn II roles removed 1 January 2027.

## Retention (verified via Skatteetaten/Altinn guidance and NBS)

- 5 years primary documentation; 3.5 years supplementary; 3.5 years
  electronic accessibility (§ 13b, forskrift § 7-7); 10 years for specific
  industries in forskrift kap. 8.
- Storage abroad: forskrift § 7-5 (amended 27 January 2025) allows EEA, UK
  and Switzerland without dispensation, but written notice to skattekontoret
  is required. Backups included (§ 7-2). Sources: Revisorforeningen
  "Enklere å oppbevare regnskapsmateriale i utlandet"; Sticos "Regnskapsmateriale
  i utlandet – nye regler for oppbevaring"; KPMG "Endringer i bokføringsforskriften".
